Our Commitment

SAFETY FIRST

Every player, every session — safe, welcoming and fully inclusive. We hold ourselves to the highest safeguarding standards.

✓
DBS Checked Coaches
All coaches hold a valid Enhanced DBS check, renewed regularly in line with best practice guidelines.
✓
Safeguarding Trained Staff
Our entire coaching team is trained in safeguarding awareness and child protection procedures.
✓
First Aid Qualified
Coaches are first aid certified so every session is covered by trained, qualified first responders.
✓
Safe & Inclusive Environment
We welcome players of all backgrounds, abilities and ages — zero tolerance for discrimination of any kind.
